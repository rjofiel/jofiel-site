## a gift

this trip was a gift from my parents.  
plotted with one of my best friends.  
they love scheming together hahaha.

who would have thought i'd leave spain this many times this year.  
at first i wanted to explore more of the peninsula.  
and i ended up looking outside hahaha.  
(we'll go back to galicia)

## brussels

i liked brussels.  
i was expecting some super city, ultra advanced.  
but it reminded me a lot of barcelona and galicia.

galicia because of the green.  
so much nature around,  
and the state of some areas and buildings.  
that contrast between nature and buildings, i really like it.

barcelona because of the trash, even if it's tidy.  
homeless people on the street, with that cold. no idea how they hold up.  
lots of pakistani people too,  
and corner shops just like the ones in barcelona.

then the cold.  
holy shit, the fucking cold hahaha.  
now i get why they're always with beers and wrapped up.

## lost in the green

like in bosnia, little time in the city.  
i went to the green areas.  
getting lost in parks, trying some walks through the nature reserves.

scheutbos, neerpede, and a bit of vogelzang.  
it was beautiful.  
in neerpede they were trying to grow mushrooms,  
so they warned you to be careful with the mycelium when walking.

easy trails only.  
winter, rain, and no gear for it.  
walking in jordans through paths full of mud.  
a miracle i didn't fall 200 times hahaha.

nothing hard, obviously.  
more walking with no direction,  
looking at animals, plants,  
and breathing all that air.

## dark at 4

what broke me was the cold.  
and that by 4 it was already night,  
and people just disappeared.  
apart from bars and the center, there was nobody outside.

i had a cold and didn't feel like drinking,  
not right now anyway.  
so from 8 pm on i stayed in,  
nice and warm.

## luxembourg

they recommended amsterdam, to see more than brussels.  
but i already had a tour to luxembourg.  
short, but i loved it.

we left early, at 8 am.  
first dinant.  
i was freezing there,  
with the fog, the road,  
and them explaining a bit of the second world war  
and some curiosities about the town.  
it was nice.

then luxembourg.  
there they told us more about the history and what it meant.  
obviously i don't remember anything hahaha.  
or i do, but i switched off when they let us go,  
because there was a christmas market right next to us.

little time, so it was just enjoying it.  
grabbing gifts for the people in spain, time caught up with me hahaha.  
snacking around and drinking warm wine hahaha.

and that was the first day i saw the sun.  
so beautiful.  
i think brussels would gain a lot with some sun.  
i had it all cloudy and rainy, which i don't mind,  
but just to see a sunset there, it would be beautiful.

## frites

the food cost a kidney and a half hahaha.  
my first restaurant was a direct hit to the wallet.  
and since fries, french fries, frites, are from there...  
potatoes almost everywhere.  
the brasseries were good though.

## welp

short trip.  
i really liked the gift.  
in the end i get more experiences out of it.  
love you and thank you for this,  
even if i doubt you'll ever read it hahaha.

next time, amsterdam maybe.  
or some country like bosnia,  
because bosnia obviously wins (i saw more cats).
