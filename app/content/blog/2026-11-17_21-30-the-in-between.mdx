## uf, finally

the first feeling was... relief.

uf.  
finally i can rest.  
distract myself.  
do the hobbies i couldn't do before.

and i did.  
for a while.

## the sepe

first lesson nobody tells you:  
there's a date you have to remember.

fifteen working days from your last day worked.  
that's the window to ask for the unemployment benefit.  
if you go late, you start losing days.

so yes, paperwork came first.

## bored

the relief didn't last long.

after a while i was bored.  
not because there was nothing to do.  
because i didn't feel productive.

i wanted to do things.  
build things.  
feel useful again.

so the interviews started.

## the interviews

and they were endless.

phase after phase.  
call, test, another call, another phase...  
honestly, some of them gave me more laziness than anything else hahaha.

and it was harder because i wasn't home.  
i had just moved to barcelona.  
new city, no job, far from my hometown.

that made the whole transition more complicated than it should have been.

and then, the question that always comes:  
"why did you leave evalion?"

the honest answer is the short one.  
the business changed, the team was restructured.  
it happens.

welp.  
it took a while.  
but it ended.
