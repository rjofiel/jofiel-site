## the sepe

first lesson nobody tells you:  
there's a date you have to remember.

fifteen working days from your last day worked.  
that's the window to ask for the unemployment benefit.  
if you go late, you start losing days.

so yes, first week "off" and i was already doing paperwork.  
forms, appointments, documents i didn't know i had to keep.

bureaucracy doesn't care how you feel.  
it just wants the papers.  
fair enough.

## the weird months

the calendar lost its shape.

no stand-ups.  
no deadlines.  
mondays and saturdays looked the same.

i tried to keep some kind of routine.  
wake up, apply, study a bit, move a bit.  
some days it worked.  
some days it didn't.

the savings went down slowly.  
not on anything fancy.  
just... life.

it wasn't panic.  
it was more like background noise.  
always there, even when i wasn't thinking about it.

## the interview

and then, the question.  
it always comes.

"why did you leave evalion?"

the short version is the honest one:  
the position ended.  
it happens.

the long version doesn't fit in a fifteen minute call.  
and that's okay.  
it's not hiding it.  
some things just belong to the context.

what i did talk about was what i learned.  
react, python, evals.  
and what i wanted next.

not every interview went well.  
some did.

welp.  
one of them was enough.
