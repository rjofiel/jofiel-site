## the signals

it's not like i didn't see it coming.  
i did.  
or at least, i felt it.

at evalion we were few.  
and when you're few, the signals are louder, not quieter.

things that used to happen... stopped happening.  
the math in my head stopped adding up.  
i didn't say much.  
i just kept working and kept noticing.

i don't blame anyone.  
it was a startup.  
and startups are like that.  
everything is fine until it isn't.

## the day

i won't go into details.  
it was short.  
one of those calls where you already know what it's about before anyone says anything.

the words were the usual ones.  
the ones that come with "unfortunately" and "it's not about you".

i asked a couple of practical things.  
said thanks.  
closed the laptop.

and that was it.

## the empty desk

the next day was weird.  
same desk, same chair, same laptop... for a few more days.

then the practical stuff.  
returning the laptop.  
losing access to everything, one thing after another.  
paperwork.

there's a difference between choosing to have free time  
and free time falling on top of you.

this was the second one.

the desk was still there.  
it just looked different.
