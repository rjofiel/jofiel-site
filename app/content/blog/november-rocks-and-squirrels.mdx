## la pedriza

this month we were in madrid.  
and i escaped to la pedriza.

<Photo src='november-rocks-and-squirrels/pedriza' alt='me sitting under el elefantito, the granite rock shaped like an elephant in la pedriza'>

it was harder than the other routes i've done.  
so many rocks blocking the view,  
you couldn't see where to go or how to move forward.

luckily i had komoot,  
the one i subscribed to for the lukomir route in bosnia.  
and some retirees saved me from getting lost hahaha.

madrid, and then the mountain to enjoy it all.  
it was a joy. i hope i can go back.

</Photo>

## squirrel

<Photo src='november-rocks-and-squirrels/blood-bag' alt='a red drink served in a blood bag, next to a plate of chips'>

this month i talked with squirrel again.  
so many things said, and so many still left.

i was happy to see her, and worried at the same time.  
for now, we still need some distance.

the weird part: it's so easy to connect.  
not talking to her is the hard part.

i hope we talk again later on.

</Photo>

## the mamut

then, my first 30 km, at the marcha del mamut.

i never thought walking could be deadly hahaha.  
after km 22 or 23 my feet were burning.  
every step hurt.  
my whole body burning, my legs giving up, suffering with every step.

but i enjoyed it.  
finishing was a joy,  
and getting the medal was such a high.

<Photo src='november-rocks-and-squirrels/thirty-km' alt='me lying on the ground under a banner that says ¡lo he conseguido! 30km' />

## squirrels

and there i saw several squirrels.  
and finally i could take pictures of them.  
finally hahaha.

## welp

it was a good month, apart from that.  
i suffered so much that now i want to do it again.  
what a surprise, right? hahaha  
we'll see next year hehehe.
