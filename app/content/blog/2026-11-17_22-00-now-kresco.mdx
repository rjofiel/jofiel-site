## the first monday

new job.  
new people.  
new everything.

the first days are always the same.  
accesses, repos, names, trying to remember who does what.  
pretending you understand more than you do.

and then, little by little, you actually do.

## how it feels

so... kresco.

we build ai agents.  
the ones that talk to people.  
and a lot of what's behind them.

i came from frontend.  
now i'm way more in backend, data, and ai stuff.  
it's a lot.  
in a good way, mostly.

there are days where everything moves at the same time  
and i feel like i'm juggling.  
and there are days where something finally clicks  
and i remember why i like this.

i'm learning faster than i expected.  
also getting tired faster than i expected hahaha.

but i like it.  
i feel useful.  
and after the in-between months, that matters more than i thought.

let's see how this evolves.  
for now, i'm happy to be here.
