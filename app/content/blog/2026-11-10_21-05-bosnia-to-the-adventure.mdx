## why bosnia

barcelona was starting to feel monotonous.  
i needed to disconnect a bit.  
and maybe because of work too, i wanted to get out of spain for a while.

at first i was thinking about finland, or somewhere nordic.  
for someone, i guess.  
or going trekking somewhere else.

i was doubting for months...  
and in the end i landed on bosnia.  
no expectations. good price, cheap car rental.  
and to the adventure.

## the country

i spent little time in sarajevo.  
but i really enjoyed the country.

going from village to village.  
always from here to there,  
between nature, eating, or driving.

## lukomir

i tried to do the route to lukomir.  
rain and fog didn't let me finish it.  
but welp, i did a big part of it,  
and enjoyed that whole area.

then i got lost in a cave too.  
and walked, and walked,  
between so many trees and landscapes  
that i really wanted to get lost in the middle of nothing.

## a squirrel

for the first time, i saw a squirrel.  
couldn't take a picture of it hahaha.  
but i saw one hahaha.

## cats and the camera

there were cats on every corner.  
i took a lot of pictures,  
with the camera i bought for the trip.

too bad i lost the videos,  
because the camera formatted itself hahaha.  
but welp, more reasons to go back.

## welp

i really enjoyed all of it.  
let's see where the next adventure goes.
